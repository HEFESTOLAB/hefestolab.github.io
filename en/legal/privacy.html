3. Online tools: your files stay on your device

The tools under HEFESTOLAB Tools, including IFC Drawing, run entirely in your browser. When you open an IFC file:

  • the file is read locally on your device through standard browser APIs;
  • it is not transmitted to any server of the owner or of any third party;
  • it is not stored and no copy is kept outside your device;
  • any PDF, DXF or other document you generate downloads straight to your device.

This means you can work on confidential models without their content leaving your computer. Closing the tab clears the loaded information from browser memory.

4. Cookies

This site sets no cookies for analytics, advertising, profiling or third parties. It uses no tracking pixels, heatmaps or embedded social media widgets.

It does use localStorage, a browser storage mechanism, solely to remember your theme preference and, within the tools, to keep interface state while you work. This is technical and strictly necessary storage for the service expressly requested by the user, exempt from prior consent under Article 22.2 LSSI-CE. You can delete it at any time from your browser settings.

Fonts are served from this same domain: browsing the site makes no requests to external font services or advertising networks.

4b. External libraries in IFC Drawing

To interpret IFC geometry, the IFC Drawing tool loads two open-source libraries on demand (@thatopen/components and web-ifc) from the public jsDelivr content delivery network. This means that, when you open an IFC file, your browser connects to that service, which may log technical connection data as any web request would.

Only the code download request is transferred: the contents of your IFC file are never sent to jsDelivr or any other third party. The model is then processed entirely on your own machine. If you prefer to avoid that connection altogether, a local copy of the tool can be provided to run on your own network.

5. Recipients and international transfers
